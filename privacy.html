Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy explains how XNova LLC and its divisions, Bruno Group, XNova Home Improvement and XNova Rentals ("we," "us"), collect and use information when you visit ambitiousbruno.com, call or text us, or request our services.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email, and property address.
  • Job details: what you need done, photos you send, and timing.
  • Call and text records: when you call or text our business lines, including calls answered by our AI phone assistant, we keep the conversation details and transcripts so we can follow up.
  • Forms and bookings: information you submit through our website forms or our online booking page.
  • Service records: for tax, credit or records help, the documents you choose to share with us.

How we use it

  • To respond to you, schedule appointments, and provide quotes and services
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, booking links and job updates
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations

Text messaging (SMS)

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information is used only to communicate with you about your request or service. See our SMS Terms for details, including how to opt out.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to serve you, with:

  • Service providers that run our phone, texting, email, calendar, booking and website forms (for example, our phone and AI assistant provider, Google, and our form provider)
  • Licensed professionals who perform trade work on your job, with your knowledge
  • Authorities, when required by law

We do not sell personal information.

How we protect and keep it

Confidential tax, credit and family information is never shared outside the work you hire us for. We keep information only as long as needed for the service and our records, then delete it.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us below.
